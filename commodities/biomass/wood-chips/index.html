Wood Chips (Eucalyptus / Pine / Acacia)

Wood chips from plantation-grown eucalyptus, acacia and pine in Indonesia and the wider region, supplied as fibre grade for pulp and panel-board mills and as fuel grade for biomass boilers and industrial furnaces.

Wood Chips supplier in Southeast Asia: Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam and Indonesia.

Key features

  • Plantation-grown, traceable origins
  • Fibre and fuel grades
  • Screened for uniform size and low bark
  • Phytosanitary certification and fumigation where required

Applications

  • Pulp and paper
  • MDF and particleboard
  • Biomass power and CHP plants
  • Industrial furnace and boiler fuel

Packaging

  • Containerised (40 ft HC) for smaller lots
