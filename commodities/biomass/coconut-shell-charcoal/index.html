Coconut Shell Charcoal

Charcoal carbonised from coconut shells — a renewable, high-carbon, low-ash material and the preferred feedstock for coconut-shell activated carbon.

Key features

  • High fixed carbon and low ash
  • Hard, dense structure suited to activation
  • Renewable by-product of coconut processing
  • Lump, granular and crushed sizes

Applications

  • Activated carbon production
  • Metallurgical and chemical reductant
  • Industrial and household fuel
  • Briquettes and shisha charcoal

Specifications

Fixed carbon≥75%
Ash≤3%
Volatile matter≤15%
Moisture≤10%

Packaging

  • 25–50 kg PP bags
  • 1 MT jumbo bags
  • Containerised

Distribution
