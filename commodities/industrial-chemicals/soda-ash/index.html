Soda Ash

Sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃), or soda ash, is a versatile alkali used in glass, detergents, chemicals and water treatment.

We supply dense and light grades of soda ash, as an odourless white granulate or powder with consistent quality across batches.

Key features

  • Na₂CO₃ 99.2% min.
  • Dense grade for glass and bulk handling; light grade for detergents and chemicals
  • Low chloride and iron
  • Not classified as dangerous goods for transport

Applications

  • Flat, container and specialty glass
  • Soaps, detergents and cleaning products
  • Sodium silicate, sodium bicarbonate and other chemicals
  • Water treatment — pH adjustment and softening
  • Mining and flue-gas desulphurisation
