Methanol

Methanol (CH₃OH) is one of the world’s most traded chemicals. It is a building block for formaldehyde, acetic acid and olefins, a fuel and fuel additive, and a fast-growing low-carbon marine fuel.

We supply IMPCA-grade methanol in bulk and ISO tanks, inspected at load port by independent surveyors.

Methanol supplier in Southeast Asia: Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam and Indonesia.

Key features

  • Purity 99.85% wt min., meeting the IMPCA reference specification
  • Also available to ASTM D1152 / US Federal O-M-232 Grade AA on request
  • Independent load-port inspection (SGS / Intertek / BV)
  • Bulk parcels and ISO-tank shipments

Applications

  • Formaldehyde and resins (MDF, plywood adhesives)
  • Acetic acid, MTBE and methylamines
  • Methanol-to-olefins (MTO)
  • Biodiesel production (transesterification)
  • Marine fuel and fuel blending
  • Solvents, antifreeze and wastewater denitrification

Packaging

  • Bulk chemical tanker
  • ISO tanks (approx. 20 MT)
  • IBC (approx. 800 kg) and 160 kg steel drums
