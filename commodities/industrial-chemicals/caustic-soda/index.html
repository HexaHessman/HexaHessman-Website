Caustic Soda

Caustic soda (sodium hydroxide, NaOH) is one of the most essential industrial chemicals — a strong alkali for chemical synthesis, neutralisation and pH control.

We supply membrane-grade flakes, pearls and liquid lye, quality-tested for consistent reactivity in alumina refining, chemical processing, pulp and paper, textiles, water treatment and soap manufacture.

Key features

  • Membrane-process grade, NaOH 99% min. (solid)
  • Flakes, pearls (prills) and liquid lye 32% / 48–50%
  • Low chloride and iron for sensitive processes
  • Highly reactive and hygroscopic — supplied in moisture-proof packaging

Applications

  • Alumina refining (Bayer process)
  • Chemical manufacture — solvents, plastics, dyes
  • Pulp & paper — pulping and bleaching
  • Soap and detergent production
  • Textiles — mercerisation of cotton
  • Water treatment — pH regulation
  • Biodiesel and petroleum refining
