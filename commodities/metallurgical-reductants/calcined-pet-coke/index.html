Calcined Pet Coke

Calcined petroleum coke (CPC) is green pet coke heated to around 1,200–1,350°C to drive off moisture and volatiles, leaving a high-purity, electrically conductive carbon. It is the main carbon source for aluminium anodes and a widely used carbon raiser in steel and foundry melting.

Key features

  • Fixed carbon 98.5% min.
  • Low ash and volatile matter
  • Sulphur grades from low to standard
  • Sized to customer requirements

Applications

  • Carbon raiser for steel and foundry melting
  • Prebaked anodes for aluminium smelting
  • Titanium dioxide production
  • Graphite electrodes and carbon products
