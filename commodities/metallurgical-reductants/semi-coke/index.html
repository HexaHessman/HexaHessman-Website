Semi Coke

Semi coke (兰炭) is a critical input in nickel ferroalloy production, particularly the Rotary Kiln–Electric Furnace (RKEF) process widely used across Indonesia. Acting as both reducing agent and energy source, it enables efficient conversion of nickel ore into nickel pig iron (NPI) for stainless steel.

Its cost efficiency, high reactivity and stable performance make semi coke the preferred alternative to metallurgical coke for large-scale smelters.

Key features

  • High fixed carbon content
  • Low ash and sulphur
  • High reactivity and combustion efficiency
  • Lower cost than metallurgical coke

Applications

  • RKEF nickel pig iron (NPI) smelting
  • Ferroalloy production — ferrosilicon, silicomanganese
  • Calcium carbide manufacture
  • Partial replacement for anthracite
