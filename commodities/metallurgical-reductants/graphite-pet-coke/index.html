Graphite Pet Coke

Graphitized petroleum coke (GPC) is calcined pet coke treated at around 2,800–3,000°C, converting its carbon into a graphite structure. With very low sulphur and nitrogen, it dissolves quickly in molten metal and is the preferred carbon raiser for ductile iron and high-grade steel.

Key features

  • Fixed carbon 98.5–99% min.
  • Ultra-low sulphur and nitrogen
  • Fast absorption and high recovery rate
  • Reduces slag and gas defects in castings

Applications

  • Carbon raiser for ductile and grey iron foundries
  • Electric arc and induction furnace steelmaking
  • Brake pads and friction materials
