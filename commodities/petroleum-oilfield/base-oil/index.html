Base Oil (Group I/II, SN150, SN500)

Base oils are the main component of finished lubricants, making up 70–90% of most engine, gear and industrial oils. We supply solvent-refined Group I and hydro-processed Group II base oils in the two most widely blended viscosity grades, SN150 and SN500.

Key features

  • Group I (solvent-refined) and Group II (hydro-processed)
  • SN150 light and SN500 heavy neutral grades
  • Consistent viscosity index and low pour point
  • Bulk, flexitank and drum supply

Applications

  • Automotive engine oils
  • Hydraulic and industrial oils
  • Gear and transmission oils
  • Metalworking fluids and greases
  • Process and rubber oils
