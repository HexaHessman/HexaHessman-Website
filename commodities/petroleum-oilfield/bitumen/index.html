Bitumen

Penetration, viscosity, performance and oxidised bitumen grades for road construction, roofing, waterproofing and industrial use — packed to suit every route to site.

Penetration grade

Common grades 40/50, 60/70 and 80/100 — classified by the depth, in tenths of a millimetre, a standard needle penetrates the sample. 40/50 suits warmer climates, 60/70 moderate climates and 80/100 cooler regions.
