Barite Powder

Barite (barium sulphate, BaSO₄) is a high-density, chemically inert mineral essential to drilling, chemical and manufacturing applications.

We supply powder and lump forms processed for consistent particle size and purity — in API 13A drilling grade for oil & gas, and specialised grades for chemical and filler applications.

Key features

  • Specific gravity 4.1–4.5 for weighting drilling fluids
  • API 13A drilling grade (SG 4.1 / 4.2) available
  • Chemically inert and resistant to most acids
  • Customisable grades and particle sizes

Applications

  • Weighting agent in oil & gas drilling fluids
  • Filler in paints, rubber and plastics
  • Radiation shielding
  • Barium chemicals
