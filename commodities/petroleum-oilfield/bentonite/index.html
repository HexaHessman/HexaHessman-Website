Bentonite

Bentonite is a natural clay composed mainly of montmorillonite, known for exceptional water absorption and swelling. We supply powder and lump forms, grades Sec 9, 10 and 11, for drilling, foundry and environmental applications.

Key features

  • Superior swelling and water retention
  • High cation exchange capacity
  • Non-toxic and environmentally friendly
  • Grades Sec 9, 10 and 11

Applications

  • Drilling muds for oil, gas and water wells
  • Foundry sands and casting
  • Iron ore pelletising
  • Civil engineering — piling and diaphragm walls
  • Sealants, cat litter and environmental containment

Specifications

CompositionSiO₂ 55–65% · Al₂O₃ 15–20% · Na₂O 2–5%
Swelling index24–30 mL/2 g
Moisture≤10%
pH8–10
Bulk density (powder)0.7–1.0 g/cm³
Particle size (powder)2–250 µm
CAS No.1302-78-9

  • Bulk (lumps)
