Product types

Sulfuric acid (H₂SO₄) is a highly corrosive industrial chemical widely used in fertilizer production, mineral processing, petroleum refining, and chemical manufacturing. It is primarily produced from elemental sulfur through the Contact Process, with commercial concentrations typically ranging from 93% to 98%.

Global demand is driven largely by phosphate fertilizer production and mining operations, particularly nickel extraction through high-pressure acid leaching (HPAL). Due to its hazardous nature, sulfuric acid requires specialized storage, transportation, and handling procedures to ensure safety and regulatory compliance.

Procedures

Phase I · Pre-contract

  1. LOI · Buyer states intent, including any broker commission details. The LOI must come from the entity that will open the L/C.
  2. FCO · Seller sends the offer with KYC documents.
  3. ICPO · Buyer sends the purchase order with KYC documents.
  4. SPA · Seller issues the agreement; Buyer signs it and sends it back.
  5. Proforma Invoice · Seller issues it; Buyer signs it.

Phase II · L/C and loading

  1. L/C Issuance · Buyer’s bank opens a 100% irrevocable MT700 L/C at sight that matches the SPA and PI.
  2. L/C Verification · Seller’s bank confirms the L/C, and loading preparations begin.
  3. Insurance · Seller insures the cargo before the vessel sails.
  4. Vessel and Loading · Seller nominates the vessel and loads.
  5. SGS Inspection · Seller pays for SGS to certify quantity and quality at the loading port. Buyer approves the inspector.
  6. B/L and Title · A Clean On Board B/L is issued. Title passes to the Buyer at the loading port.
  7. Documents · Seller sends the full set of shipping documents, and the vessel sails.

Phase III · Payment

  1. L/C Payment · Buyer’s bank pays at sight when compliant documents are presented.
  2. Discharge Inspection (Optional) · Buyer may inspect at the discharge port at its own cost. This cannot delay payment.

Any change to these terms needs written agreement from both parties.
