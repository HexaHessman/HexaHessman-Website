Diammonium Phosphate (DAP)

Balanced crop nutrition for strong early growth.

DAP is the world’s most widely used phosphate fertilizer — 18% ammoniacal nitrogen and 46% P₂O₅ for early root development, vigorous growth and higher yields.

Produced by reacting ammonia with phosphoric acid, DAP is a dry granular fertilizer with excellent handling and storage properties, for direct application or as a base for custom NPK blends.

Performance in the field

DAP dissolves quickly in soil moisture. Its ammoniacal nitrogen drives early shoot and leaf growth, while phosphorus promotes root expansion and energy transfer. Its initial alkaline reaction (pH > 7.5) can help neutralise acidic soils early in the season; correct placement and timing avoid nitrogen loss through volatilisation.

Storage & handling

  • Store cool and dry, away from moisture and direct sunlight
  • Compatible with most fertilizers and micronutrients for blending
  • Stable in standard storage conditions

Applications

  • Cereals — wheat, maize, rice, barley
  • Vegetables — potatoes, onions, tomatoes, carrots
  • Fruit orchards — early fertilisation for root establishment
  • Blended fertilizers — base input for custom NPK
