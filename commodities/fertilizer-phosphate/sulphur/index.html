Sulphur (Granular / Prills / Lumps)

Elemental sulphur in granular, prilled and lump form, produced to international standards for sulphuric acid, fertilizer, refining and industrial users.

Forms

Granular sulphurFormed from molten refinery sulphur into hard, uniform granules with low dust and good flow — the preferred form for long-distance bulk shipment and acid plants.
Prilled sulphurFormed sulphur with controlled particle size, high purity and low dust, widely used as feedstock for sulphuric acid, fertilizers, chemicals and rubber.
Lump sulphurCrushed solid sulphur from open stockpiles, ready for melting. Lower cost, but irregular and more prone to breakage and dust, which is why the industry favours formed sulphur for cleaner handling.

Applications

  • Sulphuric acid production
  • Phosphate fertilizer manufacture
  • Petroleum refining and petrochemicals
  • Rubber vulcanisation
  • Nickel HPAL leaching (via sulphuric acid)
  • Water treatment
