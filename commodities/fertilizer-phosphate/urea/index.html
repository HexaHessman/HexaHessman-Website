Urea

Powering agricultural productivity with precision nutrition.

Urea (CO(NH₂)₂) is the world’s most widely used nitrogen fertilizer, delivering 46% nitrogen — the highest of any solid fertilizer — in a concentrated, readily available form. It is non-flammable, water-soluble and compatible with a wide range of fertilizers and micronutrients.

Available forms

Prilled ureaSmaller round particles · quick dissolution · suited to foliar application and fertigation
Granular ureaLarger, harder, uniform particles · resists caking · suited to mechanical spreading and blends

Application & agronomic value

Suitable for cereals, fruit, vegetables, rice, corn and oilseeds — as basal fertilizer, top dressing or through fertigation. To limit nitrogen loss in warm, moist, alkaline soils:

  • Incorporate into the soil
  • Apply before rainfall or irrigation
  • Use urease inhibitors where needed

Applications

  • Agricultural fertilizer
  • NPK blending
  • Industrial — resins, AdBlue / DEF (technical grade), animal feed
