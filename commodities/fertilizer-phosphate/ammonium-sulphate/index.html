Ammonium Sulphate

Ammonium sulphate ((NH₄)₂SO₄) is a nitrogen-and-sulphur fertilizer and industrial chemical valued for purity, stability and versatility — promoting healthy growth, chlorophyll formation and protein synthesis.

We supply crystalline (fast-dissolving, ideal for fertigation and blending) and granular (free-flowing, ideal for mechanical spreading and storage) forms.

Key features

  • Crystalline and granular forms
  • 21% nitrogen and 24% sulphur, readily available
  • Acidifying effect improves nutrient uptake in alkaline soils
  • Excellent solubility and compatibility with other fertilizers

Applications

  • Agricultural fertilizer — rice, maize, oilseeds, palm oil, fruit and vegetables
  • Soil amendment — improves uptake of P, Fe and Zn in alkaline soils
  • Industrial — water treatment, textiles, food acidity regulator, flame retardants
