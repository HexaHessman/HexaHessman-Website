Phosphoric Acid

Phosphoric acid (H₃PO₄) is the key intermediate between rock phosphate and finished fertilizers such as MAP, DAP and TSP. We supply merchant-grade acid for fertilizer producers and purified technical grade for industrial users.

Phosphoric Acid supplier in Southeast Asia: Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam and Indonesia.

Key features

  • Merchant-grade (MGA) 52–54% P₂O₅ for fertilizer manufacture
  • Technical grade 85% H₃PO₄ for industrial and metal treatment
  • Consistent impurity profile for stable downstream production
  • Shipped in stainless-steel chemical tankers or ISO tanks

Applications

  • MAP, DAP, TSP and NPK fertilizer production
  • Water-soluble and liquid fertilizers
  • Metal surface treatment and rust removal
  • Detergents, water treatment and phosphate salts
  • Food and feed phosphates (food grade only)

Packaging

  • Bulk stainless-steel chemical tanker
  • ISO tanks
  • IBC (1,000 L) and 35 kg drums (technical grade)
