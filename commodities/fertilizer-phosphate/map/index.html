Mono Ammonium Phosphate (MAP)

High-analysis nitrogen and phosphorus fertilizer.

MAP (NH₄H₂PO₄) supplies readily available nitrogen and phosphorus in a concentrated, water-soluble form. Its high phosphorus content and moderately acidic reaction make it a staple wherever early crop development matters, in both conventional and precision fertilisation programmes.

Nutrient benefits

Phosphorus (P₂O₅)

  • Early root development and seedling establishment
  • Flowering and fruit development
  • Energy transfer, crop maturity and yield formation

Ammonium nitrogen

  • Early vegetative growth
  • Stem and leaf development
  • Protein synthesis and balanced nutrient uptake

Compatibility

MAP can be combined with compatible nitrogen, potassium and micronutrient fertilizers. Avoid direct mixing with strongly alkaline materials, including high-calcium products, unless compatibility has been established.
