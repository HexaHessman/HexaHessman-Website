Our story

Established in 2007, HexaHessman was founded on a legacy of engineering expertise and precision-driven solutions. Over time our vision expanded, turning specialised technical knowledge into a platform for the supply of fertilizers, reductants, chemicals and energy products.

Our leaders have always looked beyond the immediate horizon, combining engineering rigour with entrepreneurial insight. In 2024 HexaHessman unified these legacy strengths with fresh ambition, establishing itself as a reliable partner for structured supply and engineering solutions.

Today we are guided by operational discipline, strategic insight and people-first values — connecting trusted partners worldwide and delivering what our clients need with precision and dependability.

Our voyage

From a single engineering drawing to a group trading across nine markets — the course so far.

2007

The first drawing

HexaHessman is founded on a legacy of precision engineering: specifying, sourcing and commissioning equipment for marine and industrial clients who needed it done right the first time.

2011

First principals

European manufacturers of couplings, fenders and mooring systems appoint us to represent them in Southeast Asia, and our engineers train alongside theirs at the factory.

2015

Into commodities

The trading desk opens with sulphur and fertilizers, moving bulk cargo from producer to plant with the same discipline we bring to engineering — inspected, documented and delivered on schedule.

2018

A regional network

Offices open across Indonesia, Malaysia and Thailand, putting our people on site at the refineries, plants and terminals we serve.

2021

Cleaner fuels

As shipping turns to LNG, methanol and ammonia, we commission our principals’ dry-disconnect and emergency-release couplings on board, side by side with the crews.

2023

Sharing what we know

Technical seminars for terminal operators and shipowners on safe transfer, PERC control units and bunkering practice, across the region.

2024

Group reimagined

HexaHessman Pte. Ltd. is incorporated in Singapore (UEN 202419032H), uniting commodities, engineering and investment under HexaHessman Global Group.

Today

Eight offices, nine markets

From Singapore to London, our people keep cargo moving and equipment running for clients across Asia, the Middle East and Europe — in our own colours.
