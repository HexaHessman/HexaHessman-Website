ShibataFenderTeam

HexaHessman is an official Southeast Asia representative of ShibataFenderTeam (SFT), a global leader in the design, engineering, manufacture and supply of high-performance fender systems. For more than five decades SFT has safeguarded ports, harbours, terminals and offshore structures around the world.

Principal · Fender systems

Fixed fenders

SPC Cone Fender

Conical rubber body with fully rubber-embedded mounting flanges; exceptional energy-to-reaction ratio, high shear stability and no loss of performance up to 10° approach angle. Optional overload stopper.

Applications: container, bulk, oil & gas, general cargo, cruise, ferry and RoRo terminals; navy.

FE Element Fenders

Fully moulded rubber legs with embedded mounting plates — modular and compact, for mounting behind steel panels or where space is limited. Easy maintenance and replacement.

Applications: container, bulk, general cargo and RoRo terminals; navy; barges.

Cylindrical Fender

Simple installation with chains, bars, ropes or ladder brackets; reaction force and energy rise proportionally for softer berthing. OD up to 2,700 mm, ID 50–1,300 mm, lengths up to 20 m.

Parallel Motion Fenders

Engineered systems with a torsion arm so the front panel moves only parallel to its mounting — equal energy absorption at any impact level, lower reaction forces and lighter substructures.

Applications: bulk, oil & gas, ferry and RoRo terminals; monopiles; load-sensitive structures.

Roller / Wheel Fender

Elastomer unit on a rotating shaft with maintenance-free bearings, for lock entrances, dry docks and narrow or exposed passages.

Floating fenders

Pneumatic Fender

Abrasion-resistant, air-filled rubber fender to ISO 17357-1:2014; diameters 300–4,500 mm, lengths 500–9,000 mm. Low hull pressure; deflatable for storage and reuse.

Applications: ports with extreme tidal range, ship-to-ship lightering, FSRUs, temporary berthing.

Hydropneumatic Fender

Partly water-filled and counterweighted to protect submerged hulls, with adjustable draft.

Applications: submarine jetties, certain ferry types, oil rigs.

Ocean Guard Foam Fender

Closed-cell foam core with nylon-reinforced polyurethane skin; unsinkable even if damaged; diameters 500–4,500 mm, lengths 800–10,600 mm.

Applications: navy and cruise berths, ship-to-ship and ship-to-shore operations, oil & gas terminals.
