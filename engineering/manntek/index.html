Dry Cryogenic Couplings (DCC)

MannTek QCDC couplings load and unload cryogenic liquefied gases — LNG, nitrogen, oxygen, argon, ethylene — down to –196°C, connecting quickly and safely with minimal product loss and no re-tightening during cool-down. Built to ISO 21593 and ISO TS 18683.

Size1" to 8"
MaterialStainless steel
SealsPTFE

Cryogenic Breakaway Couplings (CBC)

Designed as the weakest link of the hose line or loading arm, CBCs prevent spillage and damage from drive-away or pull-away incidents. Available in breaking-pin and cable-release versions.

Size1"–8" breaking pin · 2"–8" cable release
MaterialStainless steel
SealsPTFE
Working pressure25 bar

Powered Emergency Release Coupling (PERC)

The highest safety level, with three ways to release — by ESD signal, by manual override, or by reverting to a passive breaking pin. In a blackout it falls back to a passive breakaway coupling, protecting the manifold in any situation.

Vessel Separation Device (VSD)

  • Three-wire system with pendants of different lengths
  • First wire: alarm on vessel movement
  • Second wire: initiates ESD 1 — pumps stop, ESD valves close
  • Third wire: initiates ESD 2, activating the PERC

PERC control unit

PLC versionMonitors the whole transfer operation and ensures every step and periodic test is carried out
SIL2 versionAssessed by DNV and certified to SIL2 under IEC 61511 and IEC 61508
