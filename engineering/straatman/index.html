Straatman Mooring Systems

HexaHessman is an official representative of Straatman in Southeast Asia. With more than 50 years of maritime experience, Straatman is an established manufacturer of mooring, berthing and dredging equipment. In 2024 it reorganised into Straatman Mooring Systems and Straatman Dredging & Construction.

Principal · Mooring & berthing

Coverage: Indonesia · Thailand

Quick release mooring hooks

A key safety feature for tankers, FSRUs, FPSOs and other vessels, released quickly even under load. Designed for oil, gas, chemical, iron, coal and grain terminals, with options for electric-hydraulic release, load monitoring, remote control and rope keepers.

Ship-to-Shore Link (SSL)

Enables simultaneous emergency shutdown of ship and shore during LNG, oil and chemical transfers whenever either side detects an issue. Straatman’s SafeCom SSL is customised to client needs, with standby services available.

Smart Bollard

Measures mooring-line loads in real time — critical in high waves, strong currents and gusts — and logs data to prevent line failures and breakaways.
