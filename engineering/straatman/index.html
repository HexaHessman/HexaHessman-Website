Quick Release Mooring Hooks

Quick release mooring hooks are a key safety feature for oil and gas tankers, FSRUs, FPSOs and other vessels: they can be released quickly and easily, even under load, when the situation at the berth demands it.

Straatman hooks are built for oil, gas, chemical, iron ore, coal and grain terminals, with options for electric-hydraulic release, load monitoring, remote control and rope keepers.

  • Single, double, triple and quadruple hook assemblies
  • FPSO / FLNG and offshore hooks (FPSO and FSRU)
  • Quick release towing hooks
  • Strainstall and AMTS hooks and spares

Ship-to-Shore Link (SSL)

During LNG, oil and chemical transfers the ship-shore link guards against fire, explosion and spills. Its job is to shut down ship and shore at the same moment whenever the Safety Instrumented System on either side detects an issue.

Straatman’s SafeCom Ship-to-Shore Link is built to each client’s needs, in a range of sizes and configurations, with standby services for extra reliability.

Smart Bollard

Without load data, ports have to assume maximum mooring loads. In high waves, strong currents and gusts an overloaded line can part and a ship can break away from the quay.

Smart Bollards measure the load on each mooring line in real time and log it, giving the port and the terminal a clear picture of the mooring forces and the warning they need.

Berthing Aid System (BAS)

Lasers on the quay measure an approaching vessel’s speed, distance and angle, and large displays show them to the pilot and crew so every berthing stays within safe limits. Available with a carry-on-board system for the mooring master.
